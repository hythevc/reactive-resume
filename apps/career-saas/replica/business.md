# CareerOS business proposal

CareerOS is a working title, not a cleared trademark or registered business. Proposed audience: experienced professionals who already have career experience to organize and need to coordinate tailored applications and recruiter conversations. Launch as a focused individual subscription before considering employer or career-coach teams.

## Qualitative user evidence and product decisions

The research contains a small purposive sample from **two review pages**, not a 100-review study. Reviews are attributed reports, not verified defects; no frequency ranking is justified. Specific dates identify the source observations, while page links can change.

| Evidence                                                                                                                              | Proposed fix                                                      | Validation                                                                             |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| [Teal reviews](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/tealhq.com): Steve Koonce, 21 July 2026, describes tedious tailoring                 | Reuse career facts and make job-specific versions easy to prepare | Observe five people tailoring a real application; measure time and factual corrections |

These themes motivate tests. They do not prove switching intent. No competitor reviewer appears as a testimonial in launch copy.

## Positioning and brand

Recommended position: **one factual career profile with visible control over every next step**. Useful alternatives to test are “less repetitive tailoring” and “a job-search tracker that explains its automation.” Test these against each other; do not claim the market has chosen.

Working name **CareerOS** matches the current app. Trademark/domain/store/handle checks are **not run**; do not describe it as available or clear. Candidate alternatives for a future naming screen: CareerLedger, NextRole Desk, RoleHarbor, WorkChapter. Each needs searches across USPTO/WIPO and relevant jurisdictions, web/app stores, domains and social handles before commitment. No competitor name or logo belongs in our brand.

Voice: clear, practical, calm. Say “Draft prepared,” “You marked this as applied,” and “Review these facts.” Avoid “land any job,” “beat the ATS,” “AI approved,” “guaranteed interviews” and false delivery confirmations. The product's original neutral/green interface and wordmark are ours; competitor screenshots are research references only. Retain open-source license notices separately.

## Offer and pricing hypotheses

| Offer         | Proposed price                                     | Included at a validated production launch                                                                                                    |
| ------------- | -------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Free          | $0                                                 | One career profile, resume editing/export, basic manual job/contact tracking                                                                 |
| Pro           | **$29/month**; cancellable monthly                 | More versions, explained reviews, capped writing assistance, preparation rules and expanded tracking; exact quotas set after measuring costs |
| Photo credits | Separate one-time package; target test **$15–$29** | A stated number of provider-backed outputs, clear expiry/refund terms and source-photo deletion controls                                     |

These are proposals, not purchasable services. Current plan selectors, if shown, change local demonstration state; there is no checkout or server entitlement. Do not charge for automatic application delivery until a supported provider, explicit controls and reliable confirmations exist. Avoid an unlimited-compute promise and avoid annual commitments before retention is understood.

Billing requirements: clear renewal date, self-service cancellation, receipts, published refund rules and a visible credit balance. A failed paid generation either retries safely or restores its reserved credits. Paid entitlements come from verified billing events, never a browser plan field. Job seekers may leave after finding work; measure successful exits separately from dissatisfied churn.

## Unit economics assumptions, not provider quotes

Illustrative active Pro subscriber per month:

| Item                            | Assumption                                                    | Estimated cost   |
| ------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------- |
| Subscription revenue            | $29 before tax                                                | $29.00           |
| Payment processing              | Assumed 2.9% + $0.30; verify actual region/contract           | $1.14            |
| Writing/review compute          | 300k input + 100k output tokens at assumed $3/$15 per million | $2.40            |
| Hosting/database/storage        | Allocated planning allowance at initial scale                 | $1.00            |
| Background work/email           | Allowance excluding purchased contact data and headshots      | $0.50            |
| Support                         | $2 allowance per active subscriber                            | $2.00            |
| Contribution before fixed costs | $29 − $7.04                                                   | **$21.96 (76%)** |

This model excludes salaries, acquisition, taxes, refunds, abuse, legal and compliance costs. Compute usage could vary by an order of magnitude. Photo credits need their own ledger: e.g. a $19 package with a provisional $5 generation/retry allowance leaves roughly $13.15 before support under the same assumed payment fee. This is a target scenario, not an observed margin. Set credit counts only after measuring output acceptance and refunds. If support rises to $8/subscriber, contribution falls to $15.96. At $21.96 contribution, 100 paying accounts contribute about $2,196/month before fixed costs; this is arithmetic, not traction or a forecast.

## Validation and acquisition

1. Interview ten professionals actively applying and five career coaches, recruited with consent. Bring their own anonymized workflow examples. Test whether shared context and status clarity solve a frequent problem.
2. Run an uncharged beta with a small invited group; measure activation and task completion. Collect explicit permission before using any quote or screenshot publicly.
3. Test $19/$29/$39 monthly willingness to pay with a real, clearly described offer once live services work. Measure conversion and cancellations; do not use deceptive checkout tests.
4. Publish useful resume/LinkedIn guidance and a truthful product walkthrough; seek opt-in career-coach referrals. No unsolicited mass recruiter campaigns or fabricated reviews/communities.
5. Expand provider coverage only when support load and verified delivery rates justify it. Automatic outreach is a later retained-user feature, not the initial acquisition promise.

## Metrics with definitions

| Metric                        | Definition                                                                                | Initial decision rule to test                                    |
| ----------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Activation                    | New users saving career facts + one resume version + one real job draft within seven days | Aim for ≥40%; revise onboarding if lower                         |
| Time to first useful artifact | Median time from first edit to user-approved export/draft                                 | Aim for <10 minutes; research target, not claim                  |
| Factual correction rate       | Drafts where user flags an unsupported factual statement / reviewed drafts                | Any invented credential/achievement triggers investigation       |
| Match usefulness              | User accepts role as relevant / reviewed suggested roles                                  | Segment by role/location; do not optimize raw application volume |
| Confirmed submission rate     | Confirmed external receipts / attempted approved submissions                              | Track unknown outcomes separately; no denominator exclusions     |
| Outreach health               | Accepted, delivered, bounced, opt-out and reply counts separately                         | Stop affected workflow on abnormal bounces/complaints            |
| Retention and success         | Week-4 use; cancellation reason; self-reported job obtained                               | Separate successful completion from dissatisfaction              |
| Economics                     | Provider spend, refunds and support time per active/paying account                        | Keep costs within measured plan budget before scaling            |

Targets are proposed experiments. No current customers, conversion rates or outcomes are asserted.

## Launch copy usable now

**Headline:** One career profile. Every next step.

**Description:** Shape your resume, review your LinkedIn writing, prepare a profile photo, and keep applications and recruiter conversations in one workspace.

**Current CTA:** Try the local workspace.

**How it works:** Add your career facts. Prepare the next document or conversation. Review, export and track your progress.

**Capability statement:** This version stores workspace data on your device. Writing checks and message drafts use local rules. Photo tools prepare your image locally. It does not generate AI headshots, submit job applications, send outreach, charge a subscription or sync accounts.

**FAQ:** Can I import an existing resume? Supported Reactive Resume JSON text fields can be imported and exported; review the result. Can it apply for me? This version prepares drafts and tracks stages; submission remains on the employer's site. Is my ChatGPT subscription connected? No. Live model features will use separately configured provider access. Can I cancel? No subscription is active in this version; paid launch requires self-service billing controls.

Future copy may say “AI-generated” or “automatically submitted” only for the exact verified capability and supported integration. A mobile store listing is deferred until a real native app and store/privacy requirements are satisfied. Deployment/domain/payment provisioning remain separate release actions; no live URL is claimed here.
